Ping Pong Go! integration
================================

Ping Pong Go! is a table-tennis arcade game created by Happylander Ltd.
It offers Arcade, Classic, Bug Hunt, and challenge modes. Tip Tap exposes
two of its single-player modes as two separate cards, both served from
this one mirrored bundle (selected by the ?mode= query parameter):
  - "Ping Pong Go"   (mode=arcade)  - the headline table-tennis match vs
    the CPU; ranks by the rally points the player wins.
  - "Ping Pong Bugs" (mode=bughunt) - the Bug Hunt mini-mode; ranks by the
    game's own curTargetScore.
Both scores are the game's own authoritative integers.

This build was captured from Poki's hosted version, which wraps the
original HTML5/canvas game with Poki's platform SDK. Poki's hosted page
shell, advertising stack (Google IMA, DoubleClick, YieldMo, Media.net),
analytics (t.poki.io, api.poki.com), authentication, matchmaking, and the
remote Poki SDK were NOT copied. Tip Tap supplies a local compatibility
bridge (js/tiptap-platform-bridge.js) that replaces PokiSDK with local
stubs: ads resolve as "no ad", rewarded breaks resolve as "no reward",
analytics are dropped, and only game lifecycle and the game's own score
are forwarded to the Tip Tap parent. No request leaves the app origin.

The game's original code, art, audio, and fonts are preserved
byte-for-byte. The original creator identity is preserved; Tip Tap does
not present this copied work as its own creation.

redistribute and host this title (public GitHub source + public Replit
deployment) has been obtained from the rights holder. This notice must be
preserved in every copy and deployment.
