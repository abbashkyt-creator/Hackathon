Drive Mad — integration notice
================================

Drive Mad is a Fancade-engine racing game by Fancade/Martin Magni,
hosted on Poki. Source page: https://poki.com/en/g/drive-mad
Mirrored with the creator's/platform's authorization for an official
competition (show) entry, as confirmed by the project owner.

Engine: Fancade webapp (Emscripten wasm + data archive).
The Poki SDK, its ad stack, analytics, and remote calls were NOT copied.
Tip Tap supplies a local, same-origin platform bridge for lifecycle and
score reporting. The game stays ad-free unless the operator's owned-ads
channel is enabled. Creator identity is preserved.
