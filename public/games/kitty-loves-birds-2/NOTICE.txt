Kitty Loves Birds 2 is a local Tip Tap Games mirror of the supplied Construct
runtime. Attribution in the source metadata identifies Claudiu V as the author.

The Poki page shell, advertising, analytics, identity, leaderboards, and remote
platform services are not part of this deployment. Tip Tap loads its shared
same-origin network lock before every game script so external runtime requests
are blocked.

Deployment permission and any required source-game licence remain the
responsibility of the project owner.
